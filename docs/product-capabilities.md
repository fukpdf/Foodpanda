# Product Capability Matrix

## Customer
Location/address management, discovery, search, categories, filters, restaurant/menu, variants/add-ons, cart, checkout, payment, live status, history/reorder, favorites, reviews, support and notifications.

## Vendor
Onboarding/verification, branches, hours, menu/categories/items, availability, incoming orders, preparation workflow, promotions, analytics, earnings/payout visibility, reviews and support.

## Rider
Onboarding/document verification, online/offline, delivery offers, accept/reject, navigation/location, pickup/drop-off, proof of delivery, earnings/performance and support.

## Admin
Users, vendors/approvals, riders/verification, orders, payments/refunds, promotions, reviews/complaints, payouts, audit logs, analytics and configuration/feature controls.

## Product benchmark
Mature delivery platforms generally need discovery/personalization, promotions, multiple payment options, tracking, merchant tooling, courier tooling, support, ratings and operational analytics. Features will be adopted based on requirements, security and operating economics rather than copied blindly.
